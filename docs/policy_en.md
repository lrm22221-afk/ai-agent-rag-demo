# Acme Home Goods - Customer Policy & FAQ

## Returns
You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item.
Final-sale items and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $15.
Orders over $100 ship for free with standard shipping.

## Support
Our support hours are Sunday to Thursday, 09:00 to 18:00 (Israel time).
You can reach support by email at support@acme.example or by live chat on the website.

## Warranty
All electrical products include a 12-month manufacturer warranty.
The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse or accidents.
